About Me

Dr. Shweta Jindal is currently working as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women (IGDTUW), New Delhi. She is dedicated to excellence in teaching and research, focusing heavily on modern intelligent computing systems and data-driven computational methodologies.

Teaching

  • Data Structures & Algorithms: Fundamental analysis of data design and algorithmic complexity.
  • Machine Learning: Practical and theoretical approaches to predictive modeling.
  • Database Management Systems: Structuring relational architectures and querying layouts.
