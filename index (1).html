About Me

Dr. Shweta Jindal (also published as Shweta Singhal) serves as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Information Technology at Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women (IGDTUW), Delhi. With a rich background in higher education, her teaching philosophy focuses on inspiring engineering students and mentoring advanced tech solutions.

Her foundational research explores robust software testing mechanisms, nature-inspired heuristics, optimization pipelines, and predictive analytics paradigms. She actively contributes to the university academic ecosystem as a Faculty Advisor for the ACM Student Chapter at IGDTUW alongside senior colleagues.

Teaching & Student Mentorship

Core Courses Conducted
  • Software Engineering & Testing: Structuring systematic deployment models and automated test environments.
  • Artificial Intelligence Foundations: Designing algorithmic models driven by semantic reasoning and optimization techniques.
  • Advanced Optimization & Heuristics: Formulating mathematical nature-inspired workflows to resolve complex computer science challenges.
Institutional Responsibilities
